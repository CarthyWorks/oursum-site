Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for households that want a clear picture of their finances without any cloud service or subscription.

📥

Smart Import

Drag and drop any CSV or Excel bank export. The app auto-detects encoding and guides you through a column-mapping wizard — then saves the profile so next month's import is one click.

🧠

Self-Learning Categories

Reassign a category once, and Oursum asks if you'd like to remember that rule forever. Future imports are categorized automatically — the app gets smarter with every correction.

📊

Visual Report

A daily-use home screen with summary stats, a category pie chart, and a sortable transaction table. Filter by time period, category, and amount range simultaneously.

🔀

Split Calculator

Settle up with your partner or flatmates. Define contributors with equal, percentage, or fixed splits. Pre-populated from your active report filter — no re-typing needed.

🔁

Safe Deduplication

Import the same file twice by mistake? No problem. A content fingerprint ensures each transaction is stored exactly once — no duplicates, ever.

🌍

5 Languages

English, Italian, German, French, and Spanish. Switch language, number format, date format, and currency symbol from the preferences panel — changes apply instantly.

🎨

Two Beautiful Themes

Mountain dark — alpine stone and forest greens — and Seaside light — Mediterranean coast and clear sky. Both designed for long sessions without eye strain.

✍️

Manual Entries

Add individual transactions by hand — cash, refunds, manual income — without ever needing a bank export. Full category assignment included.

📜

Import History

Every import is logged: date, filename, profile used, rows imported, overwritten, and failed. Full transparency into how your data got here.

Done in four steps.

Every month, from raw bank export to settled household costs — in under 15 minutes.

1

Import

Drag your bank's CSV or Excel export onto the app. Map columns once, save a profile, skip the wizard forever.

2

Categorize

Rules run automatically. Fix the few uncategorized rows — each correction trains the next import.

3

Review

Explore your spending with the pie chart and filters. Sort, click categories, and spot outliers instantly.

4

Settle

Open the split calculator, confirm contributors and proportions, get the settlement figures. Done.
